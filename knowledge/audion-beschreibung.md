AUDION - One-Slide Description

What is AUDION?

AUDION enables teams to chat with target audiences and validate product improvements without real user testing or surveys.

What Makes AUDION Special?

For UX Research
- Zero Setup: Upload research documents → instantly chat with AI-generated personas
- Fast Validation: Test hypotheses and validate customer journeys without real user tests
- Living Research: Personas auto-update as new research data is added

For Marketing & Product Teams
- Chat with Target Audiences: Validate features, pricing, messaging instantly
- No Testing Required: Get feedback from personas without recruiting users or scheduling interviews
- Cost-Effective & Scalable: Unlimited conversations with multiple personas, zero recruitment costs
